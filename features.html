Features

Powerful tools to help you build habits, track progress, and stay motivated.

Dragon Ember combines productivity with playful progression. Below is an overview of core and advanced features you can use to customize your experience and stay focused.

Tasks & Habits

Create recurring habits or one-off tasks and track completion with streaks and rewards.

Avatar Customization

Choose your avatar's race and customize appearance. Equip armor and weapons to change stats and visuals.

Pets & Mounts

Collect and equip pets and mounts. Pets provide passive bonuses while mounts speed up travel and grant unique perks.

Dragon Eggs & Fusion

Collect dragon eggs and combine them (fusion) to obtain rare items, exclusive cosmetics, and powerful companions.

Crafting & Upgrades

Use collected materials to craft items, unlock abilities, and upgrade your dragon’s stats.

Progression System

Level up your profile, unlock crafting recipes, and earn cosmetics as you complete goals.

Pomodoro Focus Timer

Built-in Pomodoro timer to run focused work sessions. Complete sessions to earn in-game rewards and streak bonuses.

Quests & Challenges

Join time-limited quests, compete with friends, and earn unique rewards for milestones.

Daily Ember Challenges

Complete quick, themed challenges to earn bonus loot and boost your dragon’s growth.

Boss Battles

Use your daily progress to battle bosses — cooperative and solo modes available.

Cross-device Sync

Sync progress across devices so your account stays up-to-date everywhere.

Privacy-first Analytics

We prioritize anonymized analytics — no selling of personal data.
